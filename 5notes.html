Physics: Laser & Fiber Optics Quick Sheet

1. Einstein's Coefficients Relation

Relates spontaneous and stimulated emission probabilities with radiation frequency:

A₂₁ / B₂₁ = (8π h ν³) / c³ ==> A₂₁ / B₂₁ ∝ ν³
  • B₁₂: Stimulated Absorption Coefficient
  • A₂₁: Spontaneous Emission Coefficient
  • B₂₁: Stimulated Emission Coefficient (Principle of Laser)

2. Ruby Laser vs He-Ne Laser

  • Ruby Laser: 3-Level Solid State Laser, Pulsed Beam (694.3 nm), Xenon Flash Lamp Pumping.
  • He-Ne Laser: 4-Level Gas Laser, Continuous Wave (632.8 nm), Electric Discharge Pumping.

3. Optical Fiber Parameters

Key mathematical formulas for Fiber Optics numericals:

Numerical Aperture (NA) = √(n₁² - n₂²) = n₁ √(2Δ)
Acceptance Angle (θa) = sin⁻¹(NA)
Attenuation α (dB/km) = (10 / L) * log₁₀(Pin / Pout)
