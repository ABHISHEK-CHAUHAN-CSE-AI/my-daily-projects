Electrical Engineering: Circuit Theorems Quick Sheet

1. Kirchhoff's Laws (KCL & KVL)

  • KCL (Nodal Analysis): Sum of currents entering a junction equals sum leaving. (Σ I = 0)
  • KVL (Mesh Analysis): Algebraic sum of potential differences in a closed loop is zero. (Σ V = 0)
Nodal Eq: (V1 - V2) / R1 + V1 / R2 = I_src

2. Thevenin's Theorem Steps

Converts any complex linear circuit into a single voltage source (Vth) and series resistor (Rth):

Load Current: IL = Vth / (Rth + RL)
  • Vth: Open circuit voltage across load terminals.
  • Rth: Equivalent resistance from load terminals (Short V-sources, Open I-sources).
