📖 Daily Study Notes - Semiconductor Physics

1. Intrinsic vs Extrinsic Semiconductors

Intrinsic (निज/शुद्ध अर्धचालक): Pure semiconductor (e.g., Pure Si, Ge). Number of electrons = Number of holes ($n_e = n_h$).

Extrinsic (अपद्रव्यी अर्धचालक): Doped with impurities to increase electrical conductivity.

2. Types of Extrinsic Semiconductors

N-Type: Doped with Pentavalent impurity (e.g., Arsenic, Phosphorus). Majority carriers: Electrons.

P-Type: Doped with Trivalent impurity (e.g., Boron, Aluminium). Majority carriers: Holes.

3. Key Physics Formulas

Mass Action Law: n_e * n_h = n_i^2

Conductivity ($\sigma$): \sigma = e (n_e \cdot \mu_e + n_h \cdot \mu_h)
